ScreenBrief Privacy Policy

Effective 21 August 2026. Published by Artflow Management. Questions: contact@artflowmanagement.pl

ScreenBrief is a local-first desktop application. Your recordings, transcripts, summaries and search index are stored on your own computer. We do not operate a server that receives your recordings, and we cannot read them.

What stays on your computer

All of the following live in the ScreenBrief application-data directory belonging to your operating-system user account, and never leave it unless you explicitly configure a cloud provider as described below:

  • recording and imported media files
  • the application database, including transcripts, summaries, decisions, action items and chat history
  • derived playback files, sampled video frames and the search index
  • downloaded speech-recognition models
  • rotating diagnostic logs

Transcription and speaker separation run locally by default, using models that execute on your own machine.

What leaves your computer, and only when you ask for it

Network egress of your content happens only after you have entered credentials for a provider and selected it for a feature. Until then, no recording, transcript or screen frame is sent anywhere.

Feature you enableWhat is sentWho receives it
Cloud transcription The recording audio The transcription provider you selected: OpenAI, Groq, Deepgram or AssemblyAI
Summaries, action items, speaker naming, chat The transcript text and the instructions for that feature The analysis provider you selected: Anthropic, OpenAI, Google Gemini, OpenRouter, or an OpenAI-compatible endpoint you entered yourself
Screen analysis (off by default) Sampled still frames from the recorded video The same analysis provider
Import from a public link A request to the address you pasted That address only. Connections are validated and pinned to the resolved address.
Model and GPU-runtime downloads Nothing of yours. These download application assets. The asset host

If you run a local model server such as Ollama, requests stay on your machine's loopback interface and your content does not reach a third party. If you enter your own OpenAI-compatible endpoint, your data goes to whoever operates that endpoint, and their terms govern it.

Purchases and licensing

Payment is handled entirely by Stripe. Opening the purchase page takes you to Stripe's own checkout; ScreenBrief never sees or stores your card details. Stripe processes that payment as its own controller under its privacy terms.

To activate and periodically revalidate a paid licence, the application sends your licence key and a randomly generated installation identifier to the licensing service. It sends nothing else: no recording, transcript, prompt or provider key. The installation identifier exists to limit how many devices one licence activates, and it is not derived from your hardware serial numbers or your identity.

Updates

If you installed ScreenBrief from the Microsoft Store, updates are delivered by the Store and governed by Microsoft's own terms. The Store build contains no separate update mechanism.

If you installed the version distributed from our website, the application checks a dedicated HTTPS host once per launch. The address is fixed inside the signed application at build time and cannot be changed by a setting. Such a check exposes ordinary HTTP metadata: your IP address, the application version, the platform and the processor architecture. It never carries a recording, transcript, prompt, provider key or licence content.

Crash reports and in-app feedback

Crash reporting is off until you turn it on. When enabled, a report contains the application and runtime versions, the platform, the type of failure and a sanitised stack trace. It never contains recordings, transcripts, prompts, provider keys or log contents. The "Copy diagnostics" action puts information on your clipboard and sends nothing.

When you submit feedback or a bug report from inside the application, we receive exactly four things: the moment you sent it, the application version, your operating-system family, and the description you typed, plus the name of the screen you were on if the report was sent from one. Reports are transmitted to our support inbox and are not stored on your computer. Please do not paste confidential material into the description, because whatever you type is what we receive.

How your provider keys are protected

API keys you enter are encrypted with a random master key that is itself protected by the operating system's credential store through Electron safeStorage. On Linux, ScreenBrief refuses to run with the insecure plain-text fallback. Keys found in older configuration files are migrated into encrypted storage and the plain-text copies are removed.

Redaction of personal data in transcripts

An optional filter removes clearly formatted email addresses and telephone numbers from transcript text, word-level metadata, correction history and later manual edits. The filter is deliberately conservative and is not a substitute for reviewing a recording before you export or share it.

Retention and deletion

You control retention, because the data is on your computer. Deleting a recording moves it to the application's Trash; items in Trash are removed permanently after 30 days. Deleting the application-data directory removes everything, including the database and downloaded models. Data you have already sent to a cloud provider is retained according to that provider's policy, not ours.

Recording other people

Only record or import material you are entitled to process. Where other people are captured, you are responsible for giving any notice and obtaining any consent that the law of your jurisdiction requires, before you record and before you send anything to a cloud provider. This document describes how the software behaves; it is not legal advice.

Children

ScreenBrief is a professional tool and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the effective date at the top changes with it, and the change is described in the application's release notes.
